Automotive
Concept Project · Automotive

Range Rover Evoque

The Challenge

Automotive brands often default to generic template layouts that undersell a vehicle's identity. The Evoque needed a digital presence as confident and distinct as the car itself.

The Approach

Built a dark, crimson-accented interface that mirrors the vehicle's aggressive-luxury positioning — full-bleed gallery imagery, minimal chrome, motion used sparingly to keep focus on the product.

The Outcome

A cohesive, brand-true digital experience with a gallery and features architecture ready to extend across additional vehicle lines.

Hospitality
Concept Project · Hospitality / Travel

Private Transport App

The Challenge

High-end transportation clients expect an app experience as seamless and understated as the service itself — no friction, no visual noise.

The Approach

Designed a clean, auto-layout-driven Figma system prioritizing one-handed booking flows and restrained visual hierarchy, so the interface disappears and the service takes center stage.

The Outcome

A scalable component system ready to hand off for development, built to feel effortless across booking, tracking, and account flows.

Healthcare
Concept Project · Healthcare

Appointment Booking Redesign

The Challenge

The existing booking flow buried key actions behind unnecessary steps, causing drop-off before patients could confirm an appointment.

The Approach

Mapped the full before/after user flow, cutting steps and clarifying decision points, then redesigned the interface around a simplified path to confirmation.

The Outcome

A measurably shorter booking flow with clearer decision points at every step — a case study in systems thinking as much as visual design.

Real Estate
Concept Project · Real Estate

Estate Listings Platform

The Challenge

Luxury real estate listings are frequently over-designed with clutter that competes with the properties themselves, undermining the sense of exclusivity buyers expect.

The Approach

Designed a minimal listings interface — large-format photography, restrained typography, and a quiet inquiry flow — letting each property carry its own presence without competing UI elements.

The Outcome

A listings framework built to feel like a private showing rather than a marketplace, reinforcing exclusivity at every touchpoint.
